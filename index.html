What we do

Hospital laundry, done the right way.

Every fabric we handle goes through a clinically-aware workflow — sorted by infection risk, washed at validated temperatures, and packed for sterile handover.

Bed & Ward Linen

Bed sheets, pillow covers, blankets and patient linen — washed at thermal-disinfection temperatures, pressed flat and pack-folded for ward distribution.

  • Colour-coded bagging by ward
  • Pressed & ward-pack folded
  • Same-day & next-day options

OT & Surgical Linen

Surgical drapes, gowns and OT sets receive a dedicated infection-control wash line, barrier-pack folding and tamper-evident sealing for theatre use.

  • Dedicated barrier-zone processing
  • Sealed sterile-ready packs
  • Batch tracking & audit log

Scrubs & Staff Uniforms

Doctor and nurse scrubs, lab coats and staff uniforms — softened, stain-treated and crisp-pressed so your team always shows up looking the part.

  • Stain pre-treatment & sanitising
  • Hanger-pressed finish
  • Optional name-tag tagging

Curtains, Cubicles & Drapes

Privacy curtains and cubicle drapes are taken down on schedule, deep-cleaned and re-hung — including height re-fitting and fastener checks.

  • Scheduled rotation cycles
  • Anti-bacterial finish
  • Re-hang & fitting included

Patient Garments

Hospital gowns, paediatric wear and recovery garments — fabric-softened for skin comfort, individually folded and bagged for bedside dispensing.

  • Skin-safe, fragrance-free wash
  • Individual sealed packs
  • Size-sorted distribution

Emergency & Same-Day

Unexpected surge in load? Our rapid-response line handles emergency batches with priority routing — typically returned within 6–8 hours.

  • Priority pickup window
  • 6–8 hour turnaround
  • 24×7 coordination on call

Our process

From your ward to ours, and back.

A six-stage workflow built around hospital cleanliness standards.

  1. 01

    Scheduled Pickup

    Sealed colour-coded bags collected on a fixed route — no cross-contact with clean linen.

  2. 02

    Sort & Weigh

    Sorted by infection risk and fabric class. Each batch logged by weight and ward of origin.

  3. 03

    Wash & Disinfect

    Validated thermal cycles with hospital-grade detergent and disinfectant. OT linen on a dedicated line.

  4. 04

    Dry & Press

    Calibrated drying followed by industrial pressing for crisp, ward-ready finish.

  5. 05

    Fold, Pack & Seal

    Ward-pack or barrier-pack folding. Sealed and labelled with batch and date markers.

  6. 06

    On-Time Delivery

    Returned in clean-side vehicles to your linen bay — handed over against signed delivery docket.
